Daymark support

Daymark is a personal planning app. Your plans stay on your iPhone. There is no account to reset and no server copy the developer can open.

Before you write

  • If a save looks wrong, use Recovery inside the app before deleting anything.
  • Export a backup from Settings if you need a copy off the phone.
  • The Private notebook cannot be opened without the passphrase you chose. The developer cannot recover it.
